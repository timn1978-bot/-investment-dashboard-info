Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

About this application

Investment Dashboard Dev is a personal investment research and reporting application.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth to access Google Drive files authorised by the user.

Access is used to retrieve and store investment dashboard information, historical market data and reports.

Data Storage and Security

Application data is stored in the user's authorised Google Drive storage.

OAuth credentials are used for authentication and must be stored securely.

Data Sharing

The application is intended for personal use. Google user data is not sold to third parties.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's Google account access through their Google Account security settings.
